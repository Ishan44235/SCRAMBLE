Dogfight cockpit update

Added enemy direction indicators in the cockpit view.
- Enemies inside the forward view are shown as red plane markers.
- Enemies outside the forward view are shown as red left/right arrows.
- Marker size increases as enemies get closer.
- Original retro arcade UI was preserved.
