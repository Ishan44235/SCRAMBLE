SCRAMBLE

Answer trivia to build a fighter jet.
Then everyone flies what they built.

THE SHAPE OF A GAME

1
GET IN One person hits HOST GAME on a laptop or TV. Everyone else scans the QR code or types the 4-letter room code. No app to install.
2
THE QUIZ Questions land on your phone with a timer. Every correct answer earns a jet part. You're building an aircraft, not a score.
3
THE THREAT VOTE The table marks whoever looks most dangerous. That pilot flies with a bounty on them — and an afterburner to survive it.
4
THE DOGFIGHT All jets, one screen. You fly exactly what you earned. Whoever studied hardest is in the best aircraft.

HOW YOU HOLD THE PHONE

Everything is motion. There are buttons as a backup, but the game is played by moving the handset.

  • Tap one of the four answers to submit it.
  • Rotate the phone about 50° to install a part you've won.
  • Slide the upright phone sideways to pass left or right.
  • Tap the on-screen left/right neighbor as an alternative way to pass.
  • Keep the phone upright during quiz and passing gestures.
  • Bank to turn and pitch for throttle in the dogfight. Hold FIRE for guns.
  • In Ring Run and Lock-On, tilt the top of the phone away to move down the screen and toward you to move up, like a flight stick. Prefer it the other way? Tap the ⇅ switch under the game.

PASSING, AND WHY IT COSTS YOU

Don't know an answer? Throw it at someone else. But every pass makes the question hotter: the clock shrinks and the payout multiplies.

A question that's been round the table three times is worth a fortune to whoever finally nails it. The question nobody wanted is the one that drops the rare parts. Passing is also public — everyone sees you do it.

WHEN IT GOES WRONG

ANY 3 RIGHT ANSWERS
Load a missile. They don't have to be in a row — wrong answers in between don't reset the count. You hold one missile at a time; push the phone forward (or tap MISSILE) to fire it, and the count starts again from zero. A random pilot has to tilt to evade a missile barrage; if it gets through their shield, they lose their single best part.
3 WRONG IN A ROW
You're dropped into a cockpit ring run. Tilt to fly through the rings. Miss too many and you lose a part at random.

ARCADE MODE — NO QUESTIONS

The host can pick ARCADE instead of Trivia or Study. Then the round is a ladder of five-second games, tilt only, and every level you clear earns a jet part. The higher you climb, the harder the games and the rarer the parts. Miss one and you drop a level. Five games take turns:

RING RUN
Tilt to fly through the gates.
MISSILE DODGE
Tilt to slip away from the incoming missiles.
CANYON RUN
Tilt to stay between the canyon walls. One scrape is forgiven.
LOCK-ON
Tilt onto the bogey and hold him in the reticle.
SUPPLY DROP
Tilt under the falling crates to catch them. Dodge the bombs.

Won a part that's no better than what you have? It arrives as a crate on a fuse. Claim it, or shove it to the pilot on your left or right — it goes up a tier on the way. In arcade a crate that times out is simply scrapped; it never costs you a part.

YOUR JET

Five bays — engine, guns, missiles, wings, armor — and five rarity tiers. When you win a part that beats what's in that bay, rotate the phone about 50° (portrait toward landscape) to bolt it on — in the quiz and in arcade. There's a tap button too if rotating isn't working. In arcade, your next level waits until it's fitted.

COMMON
Cold questions. Better than an empty bay.
UNCOMMON
Solid. Most of what you'll be flying.
RARE
Noticeably faster, tougher, meaner.
EPIC
Hot questions only. Worth chasing.
LEGENDARY
Red-hot questions and lucky crates. Everyone will notice the gold on your wings.

SECRET AGENDAS

Everyone draws one at the start and nobody is a traitor. Each agenda pushes you toward behaviour that looks suspicious for a different reason — which is what makes reading the table hard.

THE MAGPIE
Finish holding the rarest single part at the table.
THE DUELIST
You get one secret rival. Shoot them down, repeatedly.
THE SCAVENGER
Tear parts off anyone you down, mid-dogfight.
THE MECHANIC
Force a part swap in discussion. They get no say.
THE INSPECTOR
Strip-inspect one pilot's loadout. Score if the table ends up marking them.
THE QUARTERMASTER
Score on every part fitted by pilots you passed to.
THE FEATHERWEIGHT
Finish with three bays or fewer. Light jets turn hardest.

WINNING

Final score = your quiz or arcade points + dogfight score + agenda bonus. Kills are worth 100, and triple on the Threat. The top three take the podium on the big screen, and everyone's agenda is revealed underneath — which is when the table works out why you kept dumping questions on them.
