Motion access

This game is played by moving your phone. We need permission to read its motion sensors.

  • Tap an answer to submit it. No twist needed.
  • Slide the upright phone left or right to pass a question to that neighbor.
  • Shake for a new question (2 rerolls per round), and rotate to install an earned jet part.
  • Bank the upright phone left or right to steer in dogfights.
  • Use the on-screen pass controls if motion is unavailable.

Readings stay on your phone and in this game session. Nothing is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere else, and everything is discarded when the game ends. You can still play with the on-screen buttons if you decline.

IN THE QUIZ
  • Tap one of the four answers. Tilting does not select answers.
  • Slide left/right while holding the phone upright to pass to your left/right neighbor.
  • Or tap the left/right neighbor to pass.
  • Shake to reroll (twice per round). Rotate the phone 50° to install a new aircraft part (quiz and arcade), or use the tap fallback.
WHEN THINGS GO WRONG
  • Any 3 right answers load a missile (they don't need to be in a row). Push the phone forward or tap MISSILE to fire it; the count starts again after you fire.
  • Tilt to evade when someone fires a missile at you, or lose your best part.
  • Tilt to fly the rings after three wrong in a row.
IN THE DOGFIGHT
  • Bank left and right to turn.
  • Pitch forward for throttle, back to slow down.
  • Hold GUNS to fire.
