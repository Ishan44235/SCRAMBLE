Motion access

This game is played by moving your phone. We need permission to read its motion sensors.

  • Tilt left and right to move the falling piece.
  • Twist the phone to rotate it.
  • Flick down to slam the piece into place.
  • Flick forward at someone to pass the piece to them.

Readings stay on your phone and in this game session. Nothing is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere else, and everything is discarded when the game ends. You can still play with the on-screen buttons if you decline.

IN THE QUIZ
  • Tilt toward one of the four answers to aim at it.
  • Twist the phone like a steering wheel to lock your answer in.
  • Flick forward to pass a question you don't know to someone else.
  • Shake hard to panic-pass it to a random rival.
WHEN THINGS GO WRONG
  • Tilt to evade when someone fires a missile at you, or lose your best part.
  • Tilt to fly the rings after three wrong in a row.
IN THE DOGFIGHT
  • Bank left and right to turn.
  • Pitch forward for throttle, back to slow down.
  • Hold GUNS, tap MISSILE.
