SCRAMBLE — Simplified mode

- Power cards and handicaps disabled server-side (including host settings override).
- Power-card HUD sections hidden; original retro arcade styling preserved.
- Secret role goals shortened to one clear sentence; existing role scoring remains intact.
- Quiz missiles are earned after every 3 correct answers in a round, regardless of incorrect answers in between.
- Missile counts reset at the beginning of each round; the quiz missile button launches a target picker and starts an opponent dodge minigame.
- Only active, joined opponents can be targeted; invalid launches do not spend ammo.
- The cockpit HUD is slightly quieter without removing essential flight controls, minimap, health or weapon indicators.
- Existing 10-second Ready gesture briefings remain intact.

Automated checks: 10 pytest tests; syntax checks for all JS and Python modules.
Physical phone and live multiplayer testing are not included in these automated checks.
