SCRAMBLE - Phase Gesture Briefings

Added a clean retro-arcade instruction screen on connected phones before each quiz round, discussion, threat vote and dogfight.
- Shows only controls relevant to the upcoming phase.
- Displays a visible countdown from 10 seconds.
- Each connected active player can press READY once.
- The phase begins early when every connected active human player has pressed READY.
- Otherwise the phase begins after 10 seconds. Bots do not block readiness.
- Repeated rounds show the quiz briefing again. The existing role reveal is preserved.

- Reduce cards drawn per round and narrow the available power-up pool.
- Simplify hidden role objectives.
- Limit or combine surprise minigame interruptions.
- Keep the phone dogfight HUD focused on health, radar, enemies and weapons.

The current game's scoring, roles, cards, and dogfighting mechanics have not been removed.
